What to Include in a Technical Report

If you are reporting a problem, include only the details needed to understand it:

  • Your device type, such as a Windows PC, Mac, iPhone, or Android phone.
  • Your operating system and browser, if known.
  • The page or tool feature you were using.
  • The steps you took and what happened instead of what you expected.

A screenshot can help explain a display issue, but please hide personal information before sending it.
