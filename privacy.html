Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

At Enyecopypaste.com, we respect your privacy and are committed to explaining clearly how information may be handled when you use our website. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to visitors.

Information We Collect

Enye CopyPaste is a simple online character-copying tool. We do not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to use the main tool.

Like many websites, our hosting, security, analytics, or advertising services may process certain technical information when you visit the site. This can include information such as browser type, device type, approximate usage information, referring pages, and other technical data required to operate, secure, and improve the website.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies. These technologies can help with website functionality, visitor measurement, security, and, where applicable, advertising.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some websites or features work.

Google Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are used on Enyecopypaste.com, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable policies and user choices.

Google may use information about a visitor's interaction with this website and other websites to provide relevant advertising. Visitors can manage available advertising and privacy choices through Google's advertising settings and their browser or device settings.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for functions such as website hosting, security, analytics, performance measurement, or advertising. These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Enyecopypaste.com does not sell personal information submitted by visitors.

How We Use Information

Information that may be available to us or our service providers can be used for legitimate website purposes, including:

  • Operating and maintaining the website and its tools.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
  • Understanding general website usage and traffic.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing abuse.
  • Supporting advertising services when advertising is enabled.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

Children's Privacy

Enyecopypaste.com is a general-purpose website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights or choices concerning cookies, personalized advertising, and certain personal information. Available controls may include browser settings, device settings, and privacy or advertising controls provided by third-party services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how privacy is handled on Enyecopypaste.com, please contact us through our Contact page.
