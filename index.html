How to Type Ñ or ñ on Different Devices

The method depends on your operating system and keyboard. Here are the quickest options.

Windows PC & Laptop

On a Windows keyboard with a numeric keypad, use Alt + 165 for Ñ and Alt + 164 for ñ. Some laptops require an alternative method when they do not have a dedicated NumPad.

Mac

Press Option + N, release the keys, and then press N for ñ. For uppercase Ñ, use Shift + N at the final step.

iPhone & iPad

Open the keyboard, press and hold the N key, then slide to the ñ or Ñ character.

Android

On most standard Android keyboards, press and hold N and select ñ or Ñ from the character options.

What Is the Enye Letter Ñ?

Ñ, called eñe in Spanish, is a distinct letter rather than simply an N with decoration. Its lowercase form is ñ.

The character is widely used in Spanish words, names, and place names. It is also used in Filipino writing and appears in many digital documents, websites, messages, and usernames.

If you only need the character, the fastest option is to use the copy buttons at the top of this page. If you need to enter it repeatedly, the keyboard guides above explain the available methods for different devices.
