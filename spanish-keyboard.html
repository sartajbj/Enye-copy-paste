How to Use the Online Spanish Keyboard

1. Type or paste your text. Start by entering your Spanish text in the large editor.

2. Place your cursor. Click anywhere inside the text where you want a Spanish character to appear. You can also select text and replace the selection with a character.

3. Choose a character. Click á, é, í, ñ, ó, ú, ü, ¿, ¡ or another available character. It will be inserted at your selected position.

4. Use Shift for uppercase. Turn on the Shift button when you need Á, É, Í, Ñ, Ó, Ú or Ü.

5. Copy your finished text. Click Copy All to copy the complete text and paste it into another application.

When Is a Spanish Keyboard Useful?

An online Spanish keyboard is useful whenever your physical keyboard does not provide convenient access to Spanish characters. It can be used for writing Spanish words, names, addresses, assignments, emails, forms, social media posts and website content.

It is especially useful on shared computers, public computers, temporary devices or English keyboard layouts where switching the entire keyboard language is unnecessary.
